Aetherstars

Aetherstars Developer Agreement

Effective date: 22 August 2026

TL;DR

Aetherstars is a privacy-first game launcher and distribution platform for games made with the Godot Engine. Developers and Studios retain ownership of their games. By submitting a game, a Developer gives Aetherstars the limited permission necessary to review, host, store, distribute, display, and promote that game through Aetherstars.

Developers do not pay Aetherstars to submit or host games, and Aetherstars does not currently pay Developers for games distributed through the platform.

Aetherstars does not provide Developers with player analytics, behavioral data, click data, tracking information, or personal account information simply because someone plays their game through Aetherstars.

Games submitted to Aetherstars must currently be developed with Godot Engine 4.7.1 and must meet Aetherstars' technical, privacy, security, and content requirements.

The version of a game distributed through Aetherstars must be free to play and must not contain advertising, in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid upgrades, donations, payment systems, tracking, analytics, or other prohibited monetization or data-collection functionality.

Every game is manually reviewed before publication. Developers may publish their games on other platforms at the same time. Aetherstars is non-exclusive.
