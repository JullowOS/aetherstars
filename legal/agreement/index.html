Aetherstars

Aetherstars Developer Agreement

Effective date: 22 August 2026

TL;DR

Aetherstars is a privacy-first game platform that allows Developers and Studios to submit games created with the Godot Engine for distribution through Aetherstars. Developers retain ownership of their games and remain responsible for the content and functionality of their games. Aetherstars receives only the permissions necessary to review, host, distribute, operate, display, and promote submitted games through the Aetherstars ecosystem and related promotional channels.

Aetherstars currently does not charge Developers to submit or host games, and Developers are not paid for games distributed through Aetherstars. Aetherstars does not provide Developers with player analytics, behavioral data, click data, tracking information, advertising data, or other player activity data from gameplay occurring through Aetherstars.

Games submitted to Aetherstars must be made with the supported version of the Godot Engine, currently Godot Engine 4.7.1, and must comply with Aetherstars' technical, privacy, security, content, and other requirements.

3. Godot Engine Requirement

3.1 Aetherstars is a Godot-focused game platform. Games submitted to Aetherstars must be developed using the Godot Engine.

3.2 Aetherstars does not currently accept games developed using other game engines, including Unity, Unreal Engine, GameMaker, or other non-Godot engines.

3.3 The currently supported Godot Engine version for submissions is Godot Engine 4.7.1.

3.4 Aetherstars may change its supported Godot Engine version or technical requirements in the future. The currently supported version will be communicated through the submission interface or other official Aetherstars documentation.

3.5 A game created using an unsupported Godot version may be rejected until the Developer provides a compatible build. Aetherstars may allow additional Godot versions in the future.

3.6 Meeting the Godot requirement does not guarantee publication. Every submitted game remains subject to Aetherstars' review process.

3.7 Developers are responsible for ensuring that their submitted game complies with Godot's applicable licensing and attribution requirements and with the licenses of third-party components, assets, fonts, music, artwork, libraries, and other materials included in the game. Godot itself is distributed under the MIT License, while games created with Godot may have their own separate licenses. Developers remain responsible for complying with the licenses applicable to their own game and its components.

11. Privacy & Player Data

11.1 Privacy is a fundamental requirement of Aetherstars.

11.2 A Developer must not use an Aetherstars-distributed version of a game to collect player data outside the data flows permitted by Aetherstars.

11.3 Developers must not intentionally include:

  • analytics;
  • advertising trackers;
  • behavioral tracking;
  • click tracking;
  • session recording;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • hidden telemetry;
  • fingerprinting;
  • location tracking;
  • third-party tracking SDKs;
  • data brokers;
  • hidden data collection;
  • undisclosed network communications; or
  • similar tracking technologies

in a game distributed through Aetherstars.

11.4 Developers must not attempt to determine:

  • which buttons a player presses;
  • which menus a player opens;
  • how long a player plays;
  • what levels a player completes;
  • what items a player views;
  • what choices a player makes;
  • what advertisements a player interacts with;
  • what device a player uses for tracking purposes;
  • the player's IP address for independent tracking purposes; or
  • other behavioral information about players

through independent tracking or analytics systems.

11.5 Aetherstars will not provide Developers with player analytics or behavioral data generated through gameplay within Aetherstars.

11.6 A Developer must not attempt to bypass Aetherstars' privacy protections.

11.7 A Developer must not require players to create an additional Developer-controlled account in order to play the Aetherstars version of the game.

11.8 The Aetherstars account is the account used by the player while using Aetherstars.

11.9 A Developer may allow a player to choose an in-game display name or similar gameplay customisation where supported, but this does not create a separate Developer-controlled user account.

11.10 Developers must not transmit player account information from Aetherstars to their own external services unless Aetherstars has expressly approved that functionality.

11.11 If Aetherstars discovers prohibited tracking or data collection in a submitted game, the game may be rejected or removed.
